How Can We Help?

Thank you for playing Word Search Original Plus! If you have questions, encounter a problem, or want to share feedback, please contact us.

Email: sennookazu.games@gmail.com

For bug reports, please include your device model, iOS version, and a description of the issue. Screenshots are helpful but optional. Please do not include passwords or sensitive information.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is an internet connection required?

Core word search gameplay is designed to work without an internet connection. Advertising and consent-related services may require connectivity.

How is my progress saved?

Game progress and best scores are saved locally on your device. The App does not currently provide account-based cloud synchronization.

Can I change my advertising privacy choices?

Where required, the App provides a "Privacy Options" feature in the main menu. You can use it to review available consent choices.

What should I do if the App is not working?

Try closing and reopening the App. If the issue continues, please email us with details so we can investigate.
