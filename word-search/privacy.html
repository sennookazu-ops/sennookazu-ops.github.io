Privacy Policy

Word Search Original Plus

Effective Date: October 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use Word Search Original Plus (the "App"). The App is operated by an independent app developer (referred to as "we," "us," or "our").

1. Game Progress and Local Storage

The App stores game progress, scores, best times, and level completion information locally on your device. This information is used to save your progress and provide gameplay features.

We do not operate a separate server to collect or synchronize this game progress. Device operating system backups may handle locally stored data according to your device settings.

The App integrates Google AdMob, provided by Google, to display advertisements. Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically process information such as:

  • IP address and approximate location
  • Device or advertising identifiers, where available
  • Advertisement interactions and related usage data
  • App interactions and usage information
  • Crash reports, performance data, and diagnostics

This information may be processed for advertising, measurement, fraud prevention, service operation, and other purposes described in Google's policies. Actual processing may depend on your device settings, applicable law, consent choices, and Google's services.

For more information, please review Google's Privacy Policy and Google's advertising-related guidance .

3. Consent and Privacy Choices

The App uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to manage advertising-related consent choices where applicable. A consent message may be presented depending on your region and other applicable conditions.

Where required, you can review or change your choices using the "Privacy Options" feature available in the App's main menu.

4. Accounts and Analytics

The App does not require account registration or login. We do not operate a proprietary cloud synchronization service for game progress.

The App does not currently integrate a separate third-party analytics service such as Firebase Analytics. However, the advertising services described above may process usage and diagnostic information.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Game progress stored locally may be removed when you delete the App, subject to device backup and restoration behavior.

Information processed by Google is subject to Google's applicable retention and deletion practices. We do not directly control Google's data retention.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is a general-audience puzzle game and is not specifically directed to children. Advertising and consent handling may depend on applicable age-related requirements and regional laws.

If you have a concern about a child's privacy in connection with the App, please contact us.

7. International Data Processing

Third-party services such as Google may process information in countries or regions outside your own, subject to their applicable privacy practices and legal requirements.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy
